What it does

Built around one question: what do I still owe?

∞

A lifetime ledger

From the day you reached puberty until today, every prayer has a place. Mark whole days, months or years as done — the count always stays honest.

²

Make-ups woven into today

Choose how many days to make up per day. Qada spreads them across your five prayers, in order, so catching up never feels like a second job.

↺

Change your pace, keep your history

Start ambitious, slow down later. Lowering your daily pace never rewrites a single prayer you already made up.

◎

Prayer times that fit you

Calculated on your phone for your city, with the calculation method, Asr shadow and per-prayer minute offsets you can adjust to match your mosque.

⬤

For everyone who prays

⛨

How it works

Four minutes to set up. Then one tap a prayer.

  1. 1

    Tell it when your obligation began

    Puberty, not a fixed age. If you are unsure, pick the earliest plausible date — the app explains why over-counting is the safer mistake.

  2. 2

    Mark what you already prayed

    If you have been praying regularly for years, say since when. Everything after that date is marked as done in one go.

  3. 3

    Pick a pace you can actually keep

    One day of make-ups per day means one extra prayer with each prayer. Change it whenever you like.

  4. 4

    Pray, tap, repeat

    Under each prayer you will see the make-ups that belong to it — with the exact dates. Confirm, and the number drops.

Install

Android in a minute, iPhone in two.

Qada is a progressive web app: it installs from the browser, works offline, and needs no store account. The Android package is published on the releases page.

Android

No Play Store needed.

  1. ↓
    Download the latest APK

    Open the releases page and tap the newest .apk file.

  2. ⚙
    Allow install from this source

    Android will ask once: Settings → Allow from this source. This is the normal warning for any app installed outside a store.

  3. ✓
    Open and set up

    Four short steps, then the app works offline forever.

Questions

Short answers.

Is this a fatwa?

No. Qada is a personal tracker built on the Maliki position that missed prayers must be made up. For your own situation, ask a qualified scholar.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage, on your device. There is no server and no account. Export an encrypted backup from Settings and keep it somewhere safe.

What happens if I change my pace?

Only the future changes. Every prayer you already marked as made up stays exactly as it was, and the remaining make-ups are redistributed from that point on.

What if I do not know my puberty date?

Choose the earliest date that could be right. Counting a few extra prayers is safer than leaving some out, and you can correct the date later without losing your history.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Prayer times are computed on the device and everything else is local. After the first load the app runs fully offline.
