Features

Built around one question: what do I still owe?

▦

A lifetime ledger

From the day you reached puberty until today, every prayer has a place. Mark whole days, months or years as done — the count always stays honest.

☾

Make-ups woven into today

Choose how many days to make up per day. QadaPrayer spreads them across your five prayers, in order, so catching up never feels like a second job.

↻

Change your pace, keep your history

Start ambitious, slow down later. Lowering your daily pace never rewrites a single prayer you already made up.

☀

Prayer times that fit you

Calculated on your phone for your city, with the calculation method, Asr shadow and per-prayer minute offsets you can adjust to match your mosque.

✓

For everyone who prays

Exemption days are supported, so the count follows what you actually owe — not a generic calendar.

⛨

Private by construction

No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Backups are files you own, optionally encrypted with your own passphrase.

How it works

Four minutes to set up. Then one tap a prayer.

  1. 1

    Tell it when your obligation began

    Puberty, not a fixed age. If you are unsure, pick the earliest plausible date — the app explains why over-counting is the safer mistake.

  2. 2

    Mark what you already prayed

    If you have been praying regularly for years, say since when. Everything after that date is marked as done in one go.

  3. 3

    Pick a pace you can actually keep

    One day of make-ups per day means one extra prayer with each prayer. Change it whenever you like.

  4. 4

    Pray, tap, repeat

    Under each prayer you see the make-ups that belong to it — with the exact dates. Confirm, and the number drops.

Install

Android in a minute, iPhone in two.

QadaPrayer is a progressive web app: it installs from the browser, works offline, and needs no store account. The Android package is published on the releases page.

Android

No Play Store needed.

  1. ↓
    Download the latest APK

    Open the releases page and tap the newest .apk file.

  2. ⚙
    Allow install from this source

    Android asks once: Settings → Allow from this source. Normal for any app outside a store.

  3. ✓
    Open and set up

    Four short steps, then the app works offline forever.

Open releases

Prefer automatic updates? Add the repository URL in Obtainium and it installs each new release for you.

iPhone & iPad

Works in Safari, no developer account.

  1. ↓
    Open the app in Safari

    Tap “Try it in your browser” above, or the link below.

    The glyph is the square with an arrow, at the bottom of the screen.

  3. ✓
    Tap “Add”

    QadaPrayer now opens full-screen from your home screen and keeps working offline.

FAQ

Short answers.

Is this a fatwa?

No. QadaPrayer is a personal tracker built on the Maliki position that missed prayers must be made up. For your own situation, ask a qualified scholar.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser’s local storage, on your device. There is no server and no account. Export an encrypted backup from Settings.

What happens if I change my pace?

Only the future changes. Every prayer you already marked stays exactly as it was, and the remaining make-ups are redistributed from that point on.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Prayer times are computed on the device and everything else is local. After the first load the app runs fully offline.
