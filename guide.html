QadaPrayer — user guide

Everything the app does, in the order you will meet it. Ten minutes of reading, then it is one tap a prayer.

1

1. Install it

Android: open the releases page, download the .apk and install it (Android asks once to allow installs from this source). iPhone and iPad: open the app in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Both work offline after the first load.

2

2. The first screen asks one question

“Do you already use QadaPrayer?” If another phone already holds your history, paste the sync code and everything arrives — skip to step 4. Otherwise tap Start fresh.

3

3. Five short questions

Language, gender (for the exemption days), when your obligation began, whether you have been praying regularly since a date, and your city. Prayer times are computed on the phone; nothing is uploaded.

4

4. Today

Five prayers, each with the make-ups that belong to it. Tap a prayer, tick “I prayed”, and confirm. The number on the ring goes down immediately.

5

5. Correcting a mistake

Open the same prayer again: the sheet shows exactly what is recorded. Untick one make-up, or the prayer itself, then Save. The calendar updates with it.

6

6. The calendar

A lifetime, month by month. Tap a day to see its five prayers, or use Mark a range to record whole months or years at once — useful for the years before you started.

7

7. Progress and review

Progress shows what is left, your pace and the projected finish date. See the full review adds a streak, the last 28 days, the best and weakest day, and the last four weeks.

8

8. Reminders

Settings → Reminders. Choose a lead time, quiet hours and a sound. Android shows an “I prayed” button on the notification; everywhere else, tapping it opens the confirmation.

9

9. Backup and sync

Settings → Backup: export an encrypted file and keep it somewhere safe. On a second phone, either paste a sync code (Settings → Sync) or import the file — and choose Merge, so nothing is lost.

10

10. Worth knowing

Appearance: dark, light, sepia, midnight, and Arabic digits. Qibla: a compass that turns with the phone. Widget: today at a glance. About: the app checks once a day for a new version and can install it.

11

11. Backups, in detail

Settings → Backup → Download writes an encrypted file to your Downloads folder (on iPhone it opens the share sheet). Keep it in your own cloud. To restore, choose the file and pick Merge — the union of what you had and what is in the file — or Replace. Merge never loses a mark; Replace is for starting over.

12

12. More than one person

Settings → People. Each person has their own ledger, their own settings and their own sync code, so two people on one phone never share a count. Add a person, set them up once, then switch with a tap.

13

13. The widget and updates

Long-press the home screen → Widgets → QadaPrayer for today at a glance. In Settings → About the app checks once a day for a new release and offers Download and install; Android asks you to confirm, and your data is kept.

14

14. If something looks wrong

A mark you did not expect: open that day in the calendar and untick it — tomorrow always follows the ledger, never the other way round. The count does not move: check that the pace is not zero in Settings → Pace. Nothing loads: pull to refresh once; the app is offline-first, so the data is still there.
